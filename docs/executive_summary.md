# Executive Summary

**Period compared:** Jan-Aug 2018 vs Jan-Aug 2017 (same-period comparison).

## Headline
Revenue grew 138% (BRL 3.08M to 7.34M), but estimated profit margin fell
1.15 pp (22.76% to 21.61%). Profit still grew 126%.

## What drove it
- **Growth is volume-driven.** Orders +137%, AOV flat (+0.4%).
- **Margin erosion is mainly freight.** Freight per item rose 6.0% (BRL 19.32
  to 20.48) while average item price was flat (120.46 to 120.08), lifting freight
  from 16.04% to 17.06% of revenue (+1.02 pp). If freight is fully borne by the
  company (our assumption), this explains ~89% of the decline. COGS share moved
  only +0.13 pp (assumption-based).
- **It is not a geographic mix problem.** Margin fell in all five regions;
  region mix effect was slightly positive (+0.13 pp).

## Caveats
- The 2017 base is very low (800 orders in Jan 2017), inflating YoY growth.
  Monthly YoY slowed from +687% (Jan) to +49% (Aug); 2018 monthly orders are flat.
- Profit is an estimate based on assumed category COGS rates. The freight
  finding rests on real data; the category-mix finding rests on assumptions.
- Most of the margin decline happened during 2017 (24.9% in Jan to 21.6% in Aug);
  2018 margin has stabilized around 21-22%.

## Recommendations
1. Renegotiate carrier rates and review freight pricing, the main lever.
2. Test free-shipping thresholds or order bundling to raise items per order.
3. Shift focus from acquisition to repeat purchase as growth plateaus.

## Sensitivity
| Scenario | Margin 2017 | Margin 2018 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Freight 100% borne by company (base) | 22.76% | 21.61% | -1.15 pp |
| Freight 50% borne by company | 30.78% | 30.14% | -0.64 pp |

Margin levels depend on assumptions; the direction and the main driver (freight per item)
do not. Under both scenarios freight explains 80-89% of the decline.
